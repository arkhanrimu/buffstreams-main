Boxing Sanctioning Bodies

Boxing is governed by four major sanctioning bodies, each with their own world titles, rankings, and rules. Understanding these organizations is essential for following the sport.

World Boxing Council (WBC)

The WBC was founded in 1963 and is headquartered in Mexico City. It is the largest and most recognized sanctioning body in Boxing, with 164 member countries. The WBC is known for its green and gold championship belt, which is one of the most iconic symbols in combat sports. The WBC has been home to legendary champions including Muhammad Ali, Mike Tyson, Floyd Mayweather Jr., and Canelo Alvarez.

World Boxing Association (WBA)

The WBA was founded in 1921 as the National Boxing Association (NBA) and is the oldest of the four major sanctioning bodies. It is headquartered in Panama City, Panama. The WBA has two types of world titles: the Regular Championship and the Super Championship. The WBA has been home to legendary champions including Sugar Ray Leonard, Lennox Lewis, and Manny Pacquiao.

International Boxing Federation (IBF)

The IBF was founded in 1983 and is headquartered in Springfield, New Jersey. It is known for its strict rankings system and its commitment to enforcing mandatory defenses. The IBF has been home to legendary champions including Evander Holyfield, Lennox Lewis, and Bernard Hopkins.

World Boxing Organization (WBO)

The WBO was founded in 1988 and is headquartered in San Juan, Puerto Rico. It is the youngest of the four major sanctioning bodies and has grown to become one of the most respected organizations in Boxing. The WBO has been home to legendary champions including Manny Pacquiao, Vasiliy Lomachenko, and Oleksandr Usyk.

Undisputed Championship

An undisputed champion is a fighter who holds all four major world titles (WBC, WBA, IBF, WBO) in a single weight class. This is the ultimate achievement in Boxing, as it proves that a fighter is the consensus best in the world. The last undisputed heavyweight champion was Lennox Lewis in 1999, while Oleksandr Usyk became the undisputed cruiserweight champion in 2018 and the undisputed heavyweight champion in 2024.

Boxing Weight Classes

Boxing is divided into 17 weight classes, ranging from minimumweight (105 lbs) to heavyweight (200+ lbs). Here are the main weight classes:

  • Heavyweight (200+ lbs) - The glamour division of Boxing. Current top fighters: Tyson Fury, Oleksandr Usyk, Anthony Joshua, Deontay Wilder, Francis Ngannou.
  • Cruiserweight (200 lbs) - The bridge between heavyweight and light heavyweight. Current top fighters: Badou Jack, Chris Billam-Smith, Noel Mikaelian.
  • Light Heavyweight (175 lbs) - Known for producing some of the most exciting fighters in history. Current top fighters: Dmitry Bivol, Artur Beterbiev, Josh Buatsi.
  • Super Middleweight (168 lbs) - The weight class dominated by Canelo Alvarez. Current top fighters: Canelo Alvarez, David Benavidez, Caleb Plant.
  • Middleweight (160 lbs) - One of the most storied weight classes in Boxing history. Current top fighters: Jermall Charlo, Carlos Adames, Janibek Alimkhanuly.
  • Super Welterweight (154 lbs) - Known for producing technically skilled fighters. Current top fighters: Jermell Charlo, Tim Tszyu, Sebastian Fundora.
  • Welterweight (147 lbs) - One of the most talent-rich divisions. Current top fighters: Terence Crawford, Errol Spence Jr., Jaron Ennis, Vergil Ortiz Jr.
  • Super Lightweight (140 lbs) - A division full of exciting fighters. Current top fighters: Devin Haney, Regis Prograis, Jose Ramirez, Subriel Matias.
  • Lightweight (135 lbs) - One of the most competitive divisions. Current top fighters: Vasiliy Lomachenko, Gervonta Davis, Ryan Garcia, Shakur Stevenson.
  • Super Featherweight (130 lbs) - Known for speed and technical skill. Current top fighters: Emanuel Navarrete, O'Shaquie Foster, Hector Garcia.
  • Featherweight (126 lbs) - One of the traditional weight classes. Current top fighters: Naoya Inoue (formerly), Luis Alberto Lopez, Ray Vargas.
  • Super Bantamweight (122 lbs) - The weight class where Naoya Inoue became undisputed champion. Current top fighters: Naoya Inoue, Stephen Fulton, Marlon Tapales.
  • Bantamweight (118 lbs) - Known for fast-paced action. Current top fighters: Naoya Inoue (formerly), Junto Nakatani, Jason Moloney.
  • Super Flyweight (115 lbs) - A division full of exciting fighters. Current top fighters: Juan Francisco Estrada, Roman Gonzalez, Jesse Rodriguez.
  • Flyweight (112 lbs) - One of the most technical weight classes. Current top fighters: Junto Nakatani (formerly), Artem Dalakian, Angel Ayala.
  • Light Flyweight (108 lbs) - Known for speed and agility. Current top fighters: Kenshiro Teraji, Jonathan Gonzalez, Masamichi Yabuki.
  • Minimumweight (105 lbs) - The lightest weight class in Boxing. Current top fighters: Wanheng Menayothin, Petchmanee CP Freshmart, Thammanoon Niyomtrong.

  • Shakur Stevenson (USA, 22-0) - The WBC lightweight champion known for his incredible defensive skills and technical ability. He is widely considered one of the most skilled fighters in the world.
