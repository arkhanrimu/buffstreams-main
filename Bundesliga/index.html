Bundesliga Season Overview

The Bundesliga season runs from August to May and features 18 teams competing for the Meisterschale (championship shield). The league is known for its passionate fan culture, high-scoring matches, and some of the best stadiums in world football. The top four finishers qualify for the UEFA Champions League, while the fifth-place team enters the UEFA Europa League. The bottom two teams are automatically relegated to the 2. Bundesliga, and the 16th-place team enters a relegation playoff.

Key Bundesliga Fixtures

Bundesliga fans can look forward to several marquee matchups throughout the season:

  • Der Klassiker (Bayern Munich vs Borussia Dortmund) - The biggest rivalry in German football, drawing global audiences of over 100 million viewers worldwide.
  • Rheinderby (Borussia Monchengladbach vs Bayer Leverkusen) - The clash of the Rhine clubs, always producing high-intensity football.
  • Der Nordderby (Werder Bremen vs Hamburg/Hannover) - A historic northern Germany rivalry with deep roots.
  • Sudderby (VfB Stuttgart vs FC Augsburg or TSG Hoffenheim) - The southern German derby featuring intense local pride.
  • Berlin Derby (Hertha BSC vs Union Berlin) - The capital city rivalry between the two Berlin-based clubs.
  • Frankfurt Derby (Eintracht Frankfurt vs FSV Mainz 05) - The Rhein-Main derby featuring passionate fan bases.

DFB-Pokal (German Cup)

In addition to the league, Buffstreams also covers the DFB-Pokal, Germany's premier knockout cup competition. The DFB-Pokal features all 36 professional clubs from the Bundesliga and 2. Bundesliga, plus amateur teams, competing in a single-elimination format. The final is held at the Olympiastadion in Berlin each May. Bayern Munich has won the DFB-Pokal a record 20 times, making them the most successful club in the competition's history.

Bundesliga 2 (2. Bundesliga)

For fans who want even more German football, the 2. Bundesliga is the second tier of professional football in Germany. It features 18 teams fighting for promotion to the Bundesliga. The top two teams are automatically promoted, while the third-place team enters a promotion playoff against the 16th-placed Bundesliga team. The 2. Bundesliga is one of the most competitive second divisions in Europe, with several historically significant clubs competing for a spot in the top flight.
