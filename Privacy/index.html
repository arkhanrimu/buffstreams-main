At Buffstreams, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website at buffstreams.lol. Please read this policy carefully to understand our practices regarding your data.

1. Information We Don't Collect

Buffstreams is designed to be a simple and privacy-friendly platform. We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) from our users. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Physical addresses
  • Payment information
  • User account credentials

We do not require user registration or login to access any content on our website. You can browse and use Buffstreams completely anonymously.

2. Cookies

Buffstreams may use basic cookies and similar tracking technologies to maintain the functionality of our website and to improve your browsing experience. These cookies are primarily used for:

  • Remembering your preferences and settings
  • Website analytics and performance monitoring
  • Ensuring the proper functioning of the site

We do not use cookies to track your personal browsing habits, build advertising profiles, or share your data with third-party advertisers. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Buffstreams contains links to third-party streaming websites. We do not control these external sites and are not responsible for their privacy practices. When you click on a third-party link and leave our website, we encourage you to read the privacy policy of the destination site. We have no access to or control over the data practices of third-party websites.

4. Children's Privacy

Buffstreams is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take immediate steps to delete such information from our records. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately through our Contact Us page.

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time at our sole discretion. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "last updated" date.
