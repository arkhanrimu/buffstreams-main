Popular WWE Events to Watch

WWE Monday Night RAW

Monday Night RAW is WWE flagship weekly show featuring the biggest superstars in professional wrestling. Every Monday night delivers three hours of action-packed entertainment with matches, promos, and storyline developments.

WWE Friday Night SmackDown

SmackDown airs every Friday night and features a separate roster of WWE superstars. Known for its blue brand identity, SmackDown delivers high-quality matches and compelling storylines every week.

WrestleMania

WrestleMania is the grandest stage of them all and WWE biggest annual event. Often called the Super Bowl of professional wrestling, WrestleMania features championship bouts, celebrity appearances, and unforgettable moments.

Royal Rumble

The Royal Rumble features the iconic 30-man over-the-top-rope battle royal where the winner earns a championship match at WrestleMania. It is one of the most exciting and unpredictable events of the year.

SummerSlam

SummerSlam is WWE second-biggest premium live event, held every August. Known as the biggest party of the summer, it features major championship matches and marquee showdowns.

Survivor Series

Survivor Series features traditional elimination tag team matches and brand warfare between RAW and SmackDown. It is one of WWE original Big Four pay-per-view events alongside WrestleMania, Royal Rumble, and SummerSlam.

Money in the Bank

Money in the Bank features the iconic ladder matches where superstars compete to retrieve a briefcase containing a guaranteed championship contract. The holder can cash in at any time for a title opportunity.
